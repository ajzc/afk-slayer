Privacy Policy

AFK Slayer · Effective September 24, 2026

Who we are

AFK Slayer is a free browser game. This page explains what data we collect when you play and when you use Sign in with Google.

Guest play (no sign-in)

If you play without signing in, your progress is stored only in your browser’s localStorage on your device. We do not receive that guest save on our servers.

Sign in with Google

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google sends us an ID token. We verify it and store:

  • Your Google account ID (sub)
  • Your email address

We do not store your Google display name or profile picture, even if they appear in the token.

Cloud saves and session

When you are signed in we store:

  • Your game save data (progress, settings inside the save, and related game state)
  • A session cookie (afk_session) that is HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax, used to keep you signed in

Save data and related account records are hosted on Cloudflare (D1 database and KV storage) for the AFK Slayer Worker.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads in the game.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs in the game client.

Cloudflare, as our host, may process standard technical request data (such as IP address and timestamps) to operate the service, including rate limiting sign-in attempts.

Cookies

The only first-party cookie we set for accounts is the session cookie described above. Guests do not need it.

Data retention and deletion

Cloud saves remain while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and cloud save, email ajchapman20@gmail.com from the address on the account (or clearly identify the Google email you used). We will delete or anonymize stored account and save records associated with that sign-in.

Children

AFK Slayer is a general-audience browser game. Do not sign in with Google if you are not allowed to use Google’s services in your region.
